## Key points

- The product must be accessible to all age groups with a fashionable, trendy aesthetic.
- The control should be uncluttered, easy to find, and not easily lost with low-cost cues.
- A flip-open design with an electronic panel is proposed to reduce size and add features.
- The control is limited to TV and video, not combined controls, to simplify use and cost.
- Ergonomics must adapt to different country sizes with potential for larger or smaller controls.
- A touch-screen interface is considered as a wear-resistant alternative for younger, office users.
- The remote should avoid generic designs and have a unique, ergonomic shape like a banana or boat curve.
- The manual should be user-friendly, avoiding dense text and frequent searching.
