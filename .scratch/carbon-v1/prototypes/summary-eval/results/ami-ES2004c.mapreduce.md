## Key points

- Voice recognition may be unfeasible, so a whistle could be considered.
- Kinetic charging with watch-type batteries is the chosen method.
- L_C_D_ display on the top of the remote is confirmed.
- Rubberised buttons on the bottom are confirmed.
- Fruit and vegetable colours will be used for the rubber cover.
- The case itself is plastic, with a moulded design allowing for changes.
- Incorporating the company logo in the design is planned.
- Discuss swapping the position of frequently used buttons from top to bottom for thumb-friendly use.
