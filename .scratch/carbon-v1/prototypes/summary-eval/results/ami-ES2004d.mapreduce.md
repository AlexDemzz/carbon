## Key points

- The prototype meets most evaluation criteria, with repetitive strain injury being a concern.

- The kinetic battery concept is being reconsidered, possibly shifting to standard batteries.
- Voice recognition is not mandatory for the base product, with an offer as an upgrade.
- The prototype cost is under budget at twelve point five Euros.
