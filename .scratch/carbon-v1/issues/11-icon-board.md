## Question

What do Carbon's app icon (macOS 26 style) and menu bar template icon (idle and red-dot recording states) look like? Produce a board of 3–4 directions around the "carbon copy" idea to pick from.

## Answer

- **Mark: "Graphene, ultra soft"** (board direction C4). Three cells of the graphene lattice drawn as one soft line: near-circular corners, round valleys between cells, and the three edges meeting at the centre stopping short on round caps around a solid central dot.
- **App icon**: that mark in carbon black on a white background, flat, one colour, no gradient. White is provisional: the final icon is built in Icon Composer, which decides background and Liquid Glass layering.
- **Menu bar**: the same mark as an 18 pt template glyph. While recording, only the central dot turns red (systemRed), so the recording glyph is a non-template image. Nothing else changes between idle and recording.
- **Rejected**: round 1's glossy "carbon copy" metaphors (carbon sheet, voice to words, double C); round 2's hexagon + voice bars, benzene, periodic-table tile and diamond; sharper graphene takes (masked gap, rounded corners only, rounded ends only).
- Source SVGs: [`prototypes/icon-board/final/`](../prototypes/icon-board/final/) (`app-icon-mark.svg` on a 1024 grid, `menubar-idle.svg`, `menubar-recording.svg`). The geometry is generated by `softCappedLattice` in the board, if the proportions need retuning.

## Progress (2026-10-27)

- Round 1 (`round-1.html`, glossy, "carbon copy" metaphors): rejected. The author wants a flat, minimal icon; only the Graphene ring's flat menu bar glyph appealed.
- Round 2 (`index.html`): five flat one-color directions inspired by carbon itself (ring + voice, benzene, graphene, element tile, diamond). Awaiting a pick.
- Author picked C (Graphene) and asked for a central dot with a gap around it, which turns red while recording. Applied to C in `index.html`.
- Author asked for a softened version (C2, rounded corners) and one where the gap comes from edges ending early on round caps (C3).
- Author kept C3 (rounded ends) and asked for an ultra soft take on it: C4.
- Author picked C4 (Graphene, ultra soft). Fixed its geometry: cells are now open cap-to-cap paths, so neighbouring strokes overlap exactly at the round ends (the masked version split them).
- Smoothed C4: cubic near-circular corners instead of quadratic (no apex point), and a fillet in each valley between two cells (no V).
